## What’s in this release

This build brings the project to a modular, upgradable **Diamond** architecture:
- **Core facets**: Ownership / Admin, DiamondCut & Loupe
- **Game/NFT facets**: NFT Core, Royalty, Proposals (funding + voting gated mints), Marketplace, Guild & Soul mechanics, Main/Funding Tournaments
- **Accounting**: royalties are routed to the **contract** by default, so the protocol can redistribute proceeds into the internal treasuries (“tesoretti”) according to game rules and governance.

Everything runs on **Optimism (OP Sepolia)**. For value flows we use **wETH** on testnet; this keeps accounting predictable (ERC-20 semantics), simplifies marketplace operations, and makes treasuries easier to audit.

## Highlights

- **Diamond (EIP-2535)**: clean facet separation and loupe visibility for explorers and tooling.  
- **Royalties (ERC-2981)**: the contract itself is the default royalty receiver; funds are then split internally by the protocol logic.  
- **Proposals & Funding**: new collections/mints are gated by voting thresholds and donations, so creators are rewarded and the economy remains sustainable.  
- **Tournaments**: main + guild tournaments with prize distribution; tie handling is built-in.  
- **Marketplace**: list/buy with wETH and event-rich transparency.

## Developer notes

- Environment: **Hardhat**, OP Sepolia.  
- We’re standardizing on **SafeERC20** and **pull-based payouts** for safety and predictable UX.  
- Gas on L2 is friendly, but we still avoid unbounded loops; bulk/“cap” operations are either batched or handled lazily.

## What’s next

- **Gasless UX**: introduce meta-transactions (ERC-2771 via a trusted forwarder) or evaluate ERC-4337 with a Bundler/Paymaster to sponsor gas for users.  
- **On-chain game rules**: progressively migrate tournament/match logic and Soul Points updates fully on-chain for maximum transparency and third-party clients.  
- **Marketplace UI **: list/buy flows on OP, approvals/Permit, and clear transaction states.  
- **Game UI (MVP)**: tournament dashboards, join flows, leaderboards with tie handling, and champion detail pages (Soul, guild, recent matches).  
- **Playtesting on OP Sepolia**: recruit early testers, provide a short guide and test funds, collect telemetry and feedback.  
- **Mainnet deploy on Optimism**: finalize audits, set up multisig ownership, ship a canary release, and expand features behind safe flags.
